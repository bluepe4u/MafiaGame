Free Texas hold'em with your friends

A private no-limit hold'em table for your group, right in the browser. Invite friends with a link, play for free chips and replay the biggest hands. Every deck is provably fair: after the session, anyone can check that the cards weren't stacked.

  1. Open a table and share the link: 2–9 players.
  2. Set the stack, the blinds and the turn clock, then deal.
  3. After the session, check any deal and compare stats on the leaderboard.
Is it real money?

No. You play for free chips with no money value: 500 to start and a bonus every day.

What does "provably fair" mean?

Before every hand the server locks in a secret seed, and each player's browser adds its own. After the session the seeds are revealed, so any deck can be recomputed.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser on a phone or a computer.
