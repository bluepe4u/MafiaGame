Free Texas hold'em with your friends

A private no-limit hold'em table for your group, right in the browser. Invite friends with a link, play for free chips and replay the biggest hands. Every deck is provably fair: after the session, anyone can check that the cards weren't stacked.

How a game goes

  1. 01Open a table and share the link: 2–9 players.
  2. 02Set the stack, the blinds and the turn clock, then deal.
  3. 03After the session, check any deal and compare stats on the leaderboard.

Made for home games

Every deck can be checkedThe table locks each deck before the hand and your browser adds its own randomness. After the session, recompute every deal yourself.
New players welcomeYour best hand is highlighted as the cards come out, and the hand rankings are one tap away.
The story of each handAll-in odds, the card that decided it, and what the board would have been.
Chips, never moneyEveryone gets free chips to start and more every day. They can't be bought or cashed out.

Questions

Is it real money?

No. You play for free chips with no money value: 500 to start and a bonus every day.

What does "provably fair" mean?

Before every hand the server locks in a secret seed, and each player's browser adds its own. After the session the seeds are revealed, so any deck can be recomputed.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser on a phone or a computer.
