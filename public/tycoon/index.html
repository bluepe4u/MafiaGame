Tycoon: a property trading game online

Roll the dice, buy streets, build houses and hotels, and make deals with your friends until one tycoon owns the town. A classic board-game evening without the box: everything happens in the browser.

How a game goes

  1. 01Start a room and invite 2–8 players with a link.
  2. 02Take turns: buy, bid at auctions, build, and trade anything with anyone.
  3. 03The last player still afloat wins, or the richest when time runs out.

Made for long evenings

Trade anythingStreets, cash, anything, with anyone at the table.
AuctionsA street nobody buys goes under the hammer.
House rulesStarting cash, double salary, a jackpot on the free square: set them in the lobby.
A shorter gameShort on time? There's a quicker setup, and a turn clock keeps things moving.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, free and without ads.

Yes. The lobby has settings for a quick game.

No, a name is enough to play. An account keeps your stats between games.
