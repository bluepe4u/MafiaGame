Kingdoms: a card game of secret roles and city building

Each round, every player secretly picks a character, such as the King, the Thief or the Architect, and uses its power to build districts, take gold or slow a rival down. Whoever finishes a city first ends the game, and the most valuable city wins.

How a game goes

  1. 01Start a room for 3–8 players and invite them with a link.
  2. 02Pick a character in secret, then take turns in the order of their numbers.
  3. 03Build districts: once someone has seven, the round finishes and the cities are scored.
