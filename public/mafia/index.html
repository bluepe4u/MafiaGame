Play Mafia online with friends

Mafia (also called Werewolf) is the classic game of lies and deduction. The town tries to find the mafia hiding among them, and every night the mafia takes someone out. Everyone plays from their own phone or laptop, and the site is the narrator: it deals the secret roles, runs the nights and counts the votes.

How a game goes

  1. 01Start a room and send your friends the link or the code (4–12 players).
  2. 02Everyone gets a secret role on their own screen: citizen, mafia, doctor, cop and more.
  3. 03Argue it out over the built-in voice chat, vote, and find out who was right.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Nothing to download and no ads: a browser is all you need.

How many players do we need?

From 4 to 12. The site picks a balanced set of roles for your group.

Do we need a host?

No. The site runs the nights and days, so everyone gets to play.
