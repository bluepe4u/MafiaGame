Find the spy: a party game online

Everyone knows the secret location except one player: the spy. Ask each other sly questions to find out who doesn't belong, while the spy tries to work out where you are.

How a game goes

  1. 01Start a room for 3–12 players and share the link.
  2. 02Everyone sees the location and a role on their own screen; the spy sees only "Spy".
  3. 03Ask questions in turn, then vote. The spy wins by naming the location or by not getting caught.
