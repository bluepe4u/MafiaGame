Find the spy: a party game online

Everyone knows the secret location except one player: the spy. Ask each other sly questions to find out who doesn't belong, while the spy tries to work out where you are before being caught.

  1. Create a room for 3–12 players and share the link.
  2. Each player sees the location and a role on their own screen; the spy sees only "spy".
  3. Ask questions in turn, then vote. The spy wins by guessing the location.
Is it free?

Yes, free in the browser with no downloads.

How long is a round?

About 8 minutes; the host can change the timer and the set of locations.

Can we play over a video call?

Yes, or use the built-in voice chat.
