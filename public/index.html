How it works

  1. 01Start a roomPick a game. You get a four-letter code and a link.
  2. 02Send it to your friendsThey open it and type a name. No account, nothing to install.
  3. 03Play, then play againTalk over the built-in voice chat. Save your group as a table to start the next game in one tap.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no purchases and nothing to install. Poker is played for free chips that have no money value.

Do my friends need an account?

No. A name is enough to join. An account keeps stats and chips between games.

Can we play from different cities?

Yes. Every room has its own voice chat, or use your usual video call.

Which languages?

English and Russian: switch in the top bar at any time.
